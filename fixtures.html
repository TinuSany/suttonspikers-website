SVA Men's Division 3 champions, with Division 2 next.

The SVA 2025-26 season is complete. Sutton Spikers have been confirmed as SVA Men's Division 3 champions and promoted to Division 2.

Season update

Champions and promoted

Sutton Spikers finished Phase 1 in 3rd position, completed Phase 2 in 1st place, and have now been announced as SVA Men's Division 3 champions. Promotion to Division 2 has been secured for the 2026-27 season.

Final Surrey Men's Division 3 standings are available from SVA. You can view the standings here: Phase 1 standings and Phase 2 standings .

Upcoming fixtures

The SVA 2025-26 season is now complete. The 2026-27 season is scheduled to start in October 2026, with fixtures to be added here once they are published.
