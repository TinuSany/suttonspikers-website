Falcons Previous Season Achievements

2025-26 SVA Men's Division 3 champions

Sutton Spikers Falcons won the SVA Men's Division 3 championship in 2025-26 and earned promotion to Men's Division 2. The team finished Phase 1 in 3rd position before topping Phase 2.
