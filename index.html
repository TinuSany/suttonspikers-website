Why Sutton Spikers

Built on community, identity, and a love of the game.

Founded on 1 January 2025, Sutton Spikers brings together a growing volleyball community across Sutton and Surrey. The club is affiliated with Volleyball England and the Surrey Volleyball Association, and continues to build a welcoming space for committed adult players who want to train, compete, and grow together.

Latest club news

Three big updates as Sutton Spikers heads into 2026-27.

Promotion confirmed

Team 1 promoted to Division 2

Sutton Spikers finished a brilliant first competitive season as SVA Men's Division 3 champions, securing promotion to SVA Men's Division 2 for 2026-27.

AGM completed

Members approved key updates

The 2026 AGM was held on Sunday 21 June 2026 at Epsom College. Members approved Constitution v1.1, approved the accounts, confirmed officers, and agreed plans for next season.

New team planned

Team 2 planned for Division 4

Members approved entering a second team into SVA Division 4, with the option for Team 2 to operate as a mixed team subject to league rules and player availability.
